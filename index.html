How it works

Three steps to a clear picture

Enter what you know. The planner does the year-by-year arithmetic and shows where money is tight.

Add your incomes and how many children you have, and the year each was born. That is all it needs about them.

2

Your next home

School fees change what you can borrow and when

Most home planning ignores a decade of fees. This planner puts them side by side, so the question "when can we buy?" is answered with your real commitments in view.

Year by year

Your choices matter

See how staying at the current school, or moving at Year 7, changes the earliest year you could buy.

Ranges, not promises

Fees, rates and prices are uncertain over ten years, so the results show a range you can plan around.

Privacy

Built to keep your children's details out of it

School planning shouldn't need personal details about children, so the planner is being built not to ask for them.

  • No children's names. Children will appear as Child 1, Child 2 and so on, with a birth year only.
  • Your plan is yours. Each family will have its own private account. Plans are never shared between families.
  • Saved plans are locked. Saved plans are designed to be encrypted in your browser before they are stored, so no one else can read them.
