The commitment

You are not paying a bill. You are making a multi-year commitment to your loved ones.

Parents choose a school for its values, its community and the opportunities beyond the classroom: sport, music, drama, leadership, camps and service. That choice is made with love, and it often means careful trade-offs elsewhere. The fees arrive every term, usually rise every year, and overlap when more than one child is at school. Seeing the whole commitment early lets you hold that promise with confidence rather than worry, and shape your wider financial plan around it.

Many years, not one

Thirteen years of school is a long run of payments, and a long run of growing up. Seeing the whole run at once shows the total, not just this year's bill.

Overlapping children

The heaviest years are usually when two or more children are in school together. The planner shows each child by year so you can see where they overlap.

Fees and extras add up

Tuition, levies, uniforms, camps, music and sport all count, and fees usually rise each year. Build the increase in so the later years are not a surprise, and your child never has to miss an opportunity.

Visibility for better decisions

When you can see the likely commitment, child by child and year by year, you can weigh it against everything else: when to buy or move home, how much to save or borrow, and which years need extra help. Plan the whole picture, not just the next bill.

How school fees are funded

However you fund your child's education, plan it with care

Schools bill by the term, but who pays, and how, varies widely: one household or two, income or savings, an inheritance, help from grandparents, a bursary or a payment plan. Set up your own mix in the planner and see what it does to each year, not just the fees.

By the term

Four payments a year, usually at the start of each term. This is how most schools bill. The planner shows each payment and the months it lands in.

Monthly or fortnightly

Many schools let you spread the year by direct debit, often monthly, fortnightly or weekly. Smaller, regular payments are easier to fit around a pay cycle.

Pay the year upfront

Some schools give a discount for paying the whole year early, commonly a few percent of tuition. The planner shows the saving against the larger cash you need at the start.

Payment plans

Payment plan providers, such as Edstart, can pay the school for you and spread what you repay over a year or more, sometimes with a fee or interest. Include a plan's cost to compare it with paying the school directly.

Savings, inheritance and help from others

Fees are often met from savings, an inheritance, help from grandparents or other relatives, a scholarship or bursary, or shared between separated parents. Add these as deductions or extra income lines so the plan reflects who really pays.

Plan ahead, not just pay

Paying from income works until fees are at their highest. See those years early and you can decide how to cover them, before they arrive.

Payment options, discounts, fees and interest differ by school and provider. Check the terms with your school or provider. Names are examples only; this planner is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any school or payment provider.

Privacy and security

Your information stays yours

A school fees plan says a lot about a household. The planner is built so that your figures stay under your control, and the few things we do hold are protected.

Your figures stay on your device

Your plan is kept in your own browser. It is not sent to our servers, so we cannot see your incomes, fees or savings. If you choose to save a copy to the cloud, it is scrambled with your own passphrase first, so we still cannot read it.

Encrypted connection

The website and sign-in are served over HTTPS, so what you type is encrypted on its way to us.

Passwords kept safe

Your password is stored only as a salted hash, never in readable form, so even we cannot see it.

Cards handled by Stripe

The card step is run by Stripe, a leading payments provider. We never see or store your card number, and the trial costs $0.

Only what we need

Our licence service holds just your name, email address and licence dates. Nothing about your plan or your children. We count anonymous visits to this page, with no cookies and no tracking of individuals. The planner itself is not tracked.

Names are your choice

Add a child's name if you like, or keep Child 1 and Child 2. It is entirely up to you, and names stay with your plan on your device.
